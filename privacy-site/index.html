Goaliva Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Goaliva is a soccer training tracker published by Xanadu Studios. This policy explains how Goaliva handles information when you use the Android application.

Information you enter

Goaliva lets you record your optional profile name, position, age, club or team, custom skills, training templates and their position focus, practice attempts, successes, session notes, themes, and progress history. You can skip any profile question. Your position is used on your device to recommend and order templates; age is optional profile information and is not used to rank templates. Goaliva stores this information locally on your device. Goaliva does not require an account and does not upload this information to a Goaliva server.

Service providers and sharing

Goaliva does not sell, rent, or use personal information for advertising or behavioral tracking. The current version does not include advertising, third-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, account synchronization, or cloud backup operated by Goaliva.

When you use Goaliva's export or sharing feature, Android shares the information you choose with the app or service you select. That recipient's handling of the information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

Device backup

Depending on your Android settings, the operating system may include Goaliva's locally stored training data in device backup or device-to-device transfer. Those services are operated by your device platform provider and governed by its terms and privacy policy.

Information we do not collect

Goaliva does not collect precise location, contacts, photos, videos, health data, payment information, or advertising identifiers.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete individual records, clear Goaliva's storage, or uninstall the app. Deleting Goaliva removes its locally stored data, subject to any backup retained by your device platform provider. You can also clear Goaliva's storage through Android settings.

Children's privacy

Goaliva is a general sports training application and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds accounts, synchronization, analytics, advertising, payments, or other services that change how information is handled, this policy will be updated before or when those features are introduced.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Xanadu Studios at support@goaliva.app.
